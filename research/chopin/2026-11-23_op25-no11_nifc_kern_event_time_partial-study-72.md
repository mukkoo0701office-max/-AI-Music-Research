### Source-encoded observations (not claims of independent facsimile verification)
- **kern measures 1–2**: left staff whole rests; right staff m.1 `(<4e 8.eL 16eJk 4e 4f`; m.2 `4e 4c 2e;>)`. The leading digit denotes reciprocal duration (4=quarter, 8.=dotted eighth, 16=sixteenth, 2=half), not MIDI. The m.1 durations 1/4+3/16+1/16+1/4+1/4 = one whole note; m.2 1/4+1/4+1/2 = one whole note. Thus a concrete **sparse melodic statement with varied rhythmic subdivision** is encoded, not a 16-note-per-bar loop.
- **Measure 3** encoded left bass `4C 8.C 16C 4C 4FF` and upper chords `4e 4c 4G`, `8.e 8.c 8.G`, `16e 16c 16G`, `4e 4c 4G`, `4f 4c 4A`. This demonstrates chordal revoicing and bass entry. These tokens are quoted from transcription; no unverified harmonic-function labels are asserted.
- **Measure 4** contains **kern split-spine `*^` and merge `*v` for simultaneous voices, including `4ryy` invisible rest, and chordal lower staff. A parser that concatenates rows into a single note stream will destroy simultaneity.
- **Measure 5** introduces `Allᵒ. con brio` and `risoluto` annotations, `*Xtuplet`, and upper `24fff 24ccc 24eee 24aa 24ddd# 24ccc` as the first six notes of a rapid group; bass starts `4AAA'`. Twenty-fourth reciprocals are **sextuplet-equivalent subdivisions**: six 1/24 whole-note events = 1/4 whole note, not six ordinary sixteenths. Do not mistake `24` for 24th-note standard notation or assume all fast events are 16ths.
- **Measure 6** starts upper `24ffn 24cc 24ee 24a 24dd# 24cc`, with chordal lower `4e 4c 4A`. The upper rapid figure and lower chordal attacks have distinct roles and event clocks. Differences in pitch spelling between m.5 and m.6 are evidence against merely copying a fixed three-note cell, but full motivic/harmonic interpretation remains unverified.

## Reusable algorithmic architecture (ORIGINAL HYPOTHESIS, not attributed as exact Chopin method)
1. **Source event layer**: preserve edition ID, measure, spine/voice ID, written pitch spelling, reciprocal duration as `Fraction(1, denominator)`, dots, tie, tuplet, chord membership, 8va and dynamic metadata. Decode Humdrum spine split/merge, null tokens and invisible rests before reconstructing event onset.
2. **Structural layer**: `Phrase(motive, harmonic_goal, bass_goal, structural_voice, register_envelope, density_envelope)`. A theme can move from a single melodic line to chordal lower voice while fast upper ornament is independent. Infer roles from attack accents, duration, registral salience and recurrence; not from number of notes.
3. **Subdivision layer**: choose attack-group sizes from phrase energy (including 6-in-a-quarter), then construct directional contour with multi-note turning points, register goals, and harmonic anchors; do not endlessly transpose a three-note pattern. Use exact rational onset arithmetic and independent voice clocks.
4. **Two-measure development**: retain at least one invariant (rhythmic grouping, interval contour or structural arrival), change at least two of density, range, harmony target, bass goal, texture, and placement of motif. Explicitly distinguish development from deliberate reprise.
5. **NCT gate**: evaluate sounding events against spelled harmonic windows and at every harmony change, including held notes; passing requires stepwise in/out directional continuity; neighbor requires departure and return; suspension requires preparation, persistence into dissonance and resolution; appoggiatura requires metric accent and resolution. If harmony unknown, label UNKNOWN/REVIEW; no guessed consonance.
