## New repository-code findings (direct code inspection)
- `chromatic_engine_v4.py` builds a 32-note two-bar line via eight four-step direction groups and checks semitone adjacency, range and pair-boundary continuity. Its `anchor` objective tests only one pitch per four-note group, against harmony indexed by `k//4` where k ranges 0..7. Thus **all eight sampled anchors are compared with harmonies[0] for k=0..3 and harmonies[1] for k=4..7**; the actual first/second-bar boundary occurs between sample groups 4 and 5, but this assumes the fixed 16-note/bar clock. No per-event chord/NCT checks or separate upper/lower voices are present. This is not an Op.25 No.8 sixth generator.
- `contour` uses `out.extend(out[-1]+direction for _ in range(4))`. Since `extend` consumes a generator lazily and `out[-1]` changes as it appends, it produces successive semitone steps (not four copies). The result is 33 events before `[:32]`; the last direction group contributes only three steps to the 32-event output. The `next_start=line[-1]+dirs[-1]` boundary rule restores the missing step in the next pair. This matters when comparing nominal 4-step groups to actual note counts.
- Study 08 acknowledges `v2` only validates its final target against a chord-tone pitch-class set, with repeated 3-note cells. Study 07 describes three independent rhythmic layers but not a general NCT/harmonic-boundary audit. These limitations remain open; they are not resolved by synthetic tests for sixths.
- `v4` score uses `-max(line)+min(line)`, penalizing overall range rather than rewarding expressive register; the chosen objective is a design choice without source validation. `PROGRESSION` is explicitly original, not Chopin's actual progression.

## Reusable composition algorithm — ORIGINAL DESIGN, not a score transcription
**Planning:** two-bar `PhrasePlan` with independent upper/lower structural arrival pitches, harmonic windows, bass goals, dynamic tension and motif invariant. Choose a sixth-texture *preference*, not a forced interval at every event.
**Generation:** make upper and lower voices separately (distinct contour goals), then align rational-time attacks; evaluate spelled sixths as letter distance (inclusive) and chromatic size separately. Permit passing/neighbor notes to disturb sixth spacing when independently justified.
**Auditing:** split timeline at every attack/release/harmony change; half-open note windows; re-evaluate ties across new harmony without reattack. Classify NCT only with same-voice before/after context, metric weight, harmonic spelling, and resolution deadline. Unknown context => UNKNOWN; unresolved accented dissonance => REVIEW; never silently PASS.
**Development:** for the following two bars, preserve a structural motif identity but change at least two independent features (e.g., harmonic destination and accent grouping). Compare reduced melodic skeletons, bass motion and intervoice dependence; mere transposition or repeated 3-note cells fails. This two-feature threshold is an engineering heuristic, not a verified Chopin compositional law.
**Exception policy:** a deliberate sustained dissonance may remain unresolved locally if a later structural resolution is documented; until then flag REVIEW, not automatic error or automatic acceptance. A harmonic window without an identified chord cannot certify consonance.
