## Encoded facts directly checked (NOT facsimile-verified)
1. m.1 upper durations: quarter, dotted eighth, sixteenth, quarter, quarter: `1/4 + 3/16 + 1/16 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 1` whole-note unit; lower staff whole rest. m.2 upper quarter + quarter + half = one whole unit; lower whole rest. Source notation includes fermata and editorial markup; not all markers are pitch data.
2. m.3 lower `4C 8.C 16C 4C 4FF` and upper chord tokens `4e 4c 4G` etc. **Space-separated kern pitches in one field are a simultaneous chord**, not three successive quarters.
3. m.4 has `*^` and `*v` (spine split/merge), `4ryy` invisible rest, and independent voices. The extra spine changes the interpretation of tab columns. A parser must track spine lineage and not use fixed column offsets.
4. m.5 upper first six notes `24fff 24ccc 24eee 24aa 24ddd#X 24cccJJ`; each reciprocal 24 is 1/24 of a whole, so six sum to a quarter. The `24` is an encoded rational duration; do not normalize to ordinary sixteenths. m.5 lower contains `4AAA'` then chordal attacks; m.6 upper begins `24ffn 24cc 24ee 24a 24dd#X 24ccJJ` with lower chords. These are directly observed encoded tokens, not claims about original engraving or a proven harmonic progression.
5. The upper figuration is not an adequate representation of the entire musical argument: structural attacks, bass, chordal supports and upper fast subdivisions must remain distinct event streams.

## Original compositional algorithm (not a claim about Chopin's intent)
Represent `Event(source_id, measure, spine_lineage, voice_id, onset:Fraction, duration:Fraction, written_pitch[], sounding_pitch[], chord_id, tie, rest, tuplet, ornaments, provenance)`.
- Parsing: apply kern manipulator lines `*^`, `*v` before interpreting subsequent fields; carry lineage, null tokens, local voice clocks, accidental state, duration dots and simultaneous chord pitches. Preserve 8va/written-vs-sounding information; reject undecodable tokens rather than guessing. Split a sustained note at harmonic-window boundaries for *analysis*, without creating a new sounding attack.
- Compositional planning: `PhraseUnit(2 bars, harmonic_goal, bass_goal, structural_voice, motive_invariant, density_curve, register_curve, cadence_goal)`. Assign upper figuration and lower structural harmony to independent voices. Create large-scale density contrast through orchestration and note-group design, not a 3-note modulo loop.
- Development: preserve at least one *declared* motive invariant; change at least two independent axes (contour/registral apex, subdivision-group shape, harmonic destination, bass motion, voice placement, density). Reprise may preserve more but must be tagged REPRISE, not DEVELOPMENT. Force each two-bar unit to have a phrase goal and verify local and larger-scale connections.
- NCT: evaluate each pitched onset against **spelled and time-bounded** harmony, and re-evaluate held pitches at every harmony change. PASSING_CANDIDATE requires same-voice directed stepwise approach and exit; NEIGHBOR_CANDIDATE requires stepwise departure and return; SUSPENSION_CANDIDATE requires preparation, persistence across harmony boundary and stepwise resolution; APPOGGIATURA_CANDIDATE requires accent, dissonance and contextual resolution. Candidate labels are not proof: chord membership, accent, voice continuity and functional context are mandatory. Unknown harmony -> UNKNOWN, not fabricated consonance.
- Adversarial tests: reject 24ths treated as 16ths, flattened chord fields, spine merges treated as serial attacks, 3-note modulo repetition without a two-bar goal, passing labels after leaps, suspensions without preparation, and invented source-verified harmonic labels.
