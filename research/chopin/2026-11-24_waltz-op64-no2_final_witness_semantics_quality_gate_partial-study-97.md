## Algorithm design: explicit original proposals (NOT transcriptions)
1. **Witness provenance**: represent `Witness(id, parent_id, medium, source_url)`; `WrittenEvent(witness, page, bar, staff, voice, onset:Fraction, duration:Fraction, spelled_pitch, tie_prev, slur_id, grace_type, confidence)`. Derive `AttackEvent` from written events *within the same witness only*. A tied continuation at contiguous time and same sounding/spelled pitch suppresses a new attack; a slur never suppresses attack. If a witness's tie is uncertain, preserve both possible attack graphs rather than choosing the musically convenient one.
2. **Harmony boundaries**: define harmony intervals [start,end) independently of voices. Audit each sounding interval against every intersected harmony, without fabricating attacks at boundaries. Label dissonance as passing, neighbor, suspension, appoggiatura, anticipation, chord extension, or UNKNOWN. Suspension requires consonant preparation and continuity into a changed harmony followed by plausible resolution; accented appoggiatura is separately classified. UNKNOWN cannot automatically pass.
3. **Two-bar development**: encode motif as structural anchors (scale degree relative to active harmony, metrical weight, contour, cadence target), then apply a controlled transform: functional sequence, rhythm displacement, register arc, texture redistribution, or chromatic inner-line. Preserve at least one structural invariant and alter at least one meaningful feature; reject an unchanged three-note cell repeated four times as 'development' unless marked literal reprise. Verify each transformed anchor against its local harmony and cadence, not MIDI transposition alone.
4. **Rhythm**: in 3/4, a monophonic bar must account for 3 quarter units, equivalently twelve sixteenths or twenty-four thirty-seconds; tuplets use rational durations. Each independent voice is audited separately, including rests, ties and chord simultaneity. Grace realization is policy-dependent, not silently allocated a fixed value.
5. **Generation order**: form and cadential plan → harmonic route and bass → contrapuntal voices → two-bar thematic transformations → figuration and ornament → cross-boundary audit → human musical judgment. Never optimize for sheer note count.

## Exceptions and negative test requirements (specified, NOT executed in 097)
- Same pitch under slur but no tie => two attacks; same pitch with a valid tie => one attack; mismatched pitches under tie => reject.
- A tie crossing a chord change => same attack identity but dissonance must be reclassified in the new harmony.
- A neighbor mislabeled as passing must fail when the structural pitch returns; an unprepared dissonance cannot be auto-certified as suspension.
- Reject a 3/4 voice with twenty-five thirty-seconds; validate tuplets using exact rational arithmetic.
- Reject a two-bar 'sequence' that is only unvaried three-note-cell repetition; test preservation of cadence target and change of harmonic function.
- Edition lineage must not cause an A1-only tie to be injected into A2 or F.
These are **test specifications**, not passed tests. Study 096 reported six executed *synthetic* tests; those do not establish integration or score fidelity.
