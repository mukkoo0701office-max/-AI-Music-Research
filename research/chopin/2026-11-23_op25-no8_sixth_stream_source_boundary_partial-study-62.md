## Generative hypothesis (ORIGINAL DESIGN, NOT A CLAIM ABOUT CHOPIN'S EXACT BARS)
**Aim:** develop coherent double-note texture without merely parallel-transposing one note stream, while preserving structural harmonic and melodic independence.

Data model: `Event(voice_id, staff_id, onset: Fraction, duration: Fraction, spelled_pitch, tie_id, accent, source_status)`; `HarmonyWindow(start,end,spelled_chord,tonal_function)`; `PhrasePlan(2bar_id, upper_goal, lower_goal, bass_goal, tension_curve, cadence_weight)`. Each window is half-open [start,end). Distinguish diatonic sixth (letter distance six) from chromatic semitone size and from inversion; check spelled pitch, not pitch class alone.

1. **Phrase-first skeleton.** Choose phrase goals and bass harmonic rhythm before ornamentation. Define at least two bars of harmonic plan, structural melodic targets and phrase-level tension. Do not infer actual Op.25 No.8 cadences from catalogue metadata.
2. **Independent lines.** Generate upper and lower members of each sixth as independent voice trajectories with separate anchors and allowable passing/neighbor movement; evaluate dyad spacing at each structural attack. Sixth is a texture preference, not an unconditional every-event constraint. Permit justified deviations and flag them for review.
3. **Multi-clock generation.** Distinguish slower harmony/bass changes, medium structural melody and faster surface subdivision. Expand 16ths/32nds only after structural voice leading is accepted; exact rational durations prevent floating-point drift.
4. **Harmony-boundary audit.** At each attack, note end, and harmony transition, evaluate both dyad members and bass. Held notes must be reclassified against the new chord without treating continuation as reattack. A note ending exactly at boundary is inactive in the next window.
5. **Nonchord-tone classifier.** Chord membership is not sufficient. Candidate passing: stepwise same direction in one voice; neighbor: departure and return; suspension: prepared tied dissonance crossing harmony boundary with contextual resolution; appoggiatura: accented nonchord arrival and contextual resolution. Check spelling, metric strength, onset, preceding/following events, and deadline. Missing context => UNKNOWN; unresolved accented dissonance => REVIEW, not silently accepted. A sixth dyad may contain one chord tone and one NCT; classify each independently.
6. **Two-bar development.** Retain a recognisable motivic anchor and vary at least two of contour, rhythm, accent phase, register, harmonic destination, voice-role or bass relation. Reject a 3-note loop repeated without phrase-level development. This is a heuristic acceptance gate, not a proven Chopin rule.
7. **Counterpoint and reduction.** Reduce fast notes to structural arrivals and assess each voice's directed shape, harmonic plausibility, spacing, unintentional perfect parallels and crossing. Do not automatically forbid parallel sixths: they are a legitimate texture. Test musical independence beyond different onset arrays.
