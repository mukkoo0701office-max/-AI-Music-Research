## New transferable algorithm — ORIGINAL proposal, not an empirical Chopin rule
**Key principle:** rapid attack count and phrase grammar are separate. A recognizable gesture is represented by a structured tuple `(attack_mask, articulation_pattern, interval_skeleton, register_targets, harmonic_target, bass_countergoal)`, not by a fixed 3-note pitch cell. A multi-note chord/octave is one simultaneous attack with multiple spelled pitches, not two consecutive notes. An articulation group may combine short and connected events; do not assume octave doublings imply independent contrapuntal voices.

1. **Phrase plan before surface notes.** Create a 4- or 8-bar phrase graph (lengths are adjustable engineering defaults, NOT score facts) with start, local apex, tension trajectory, cadence type, harmonic windows and bass arrivals. Generate 2-bar units conditioned on this graph; a unit cannot select its cadence in isolation.
2. **Rhythmic hierarchy.** Represent attack/release as rational fractions and separate `duration` from `sounding_length` (staccato); distinguish eighth/sixteenth/thirty-second subdivisions and ties from reattacks. Reject impossible overlapping same-voice notes unless explicit polyphony.
3. **Motivic transformation.** Preserve one recognizable invariant (e.g. rhythmic silhouette or interval-skeleton endpoint) while changing at least two of register, articulation, contour, harmonic goal, attack placement, or bass response. This is a diversity heuristic, not a claimed historical law. Require reduced phrase trajectories to differ; mere transposition and looping three-note cells are insufficient.
4. **Harmony/NCT.** Each note onset and each harmony boundary is an audit event. Use spelled pitches, bass, local chord and preceding/following same-voice notes to propose passing/neighbor/appoggiatura/suspension status. A tied dissonance across harmony must show preparation and actual step resolution to earn suspension label. Unavailable context => UNKNOWN; unresolved accented dissonance => REVIEW; a bare pitch-class membership check cannot certify a phrase.
5. **Counterpoint and texture.** Model bass as a distinct structural layer with its own arrival clock, not a copy of upper attacks. Detect hidden parallel perfect intervals only when genuine independent voice-leading exists; doubled octave sonorities should not be mistaken for two independent lines. Test register crossings and sounding overlaps explicitly.
6. **Exception policy.** Intentional accented appoggiaturas or delayed resolutions are permitted only with recorded resolution deadlines or explicit manual review. Cadential silence, truncated gestures and elisions are valid phrase-design options, not unconditional errors.

## Implementable checks and adversarial tests (specified, NOT run)
- `sum(event.notated_duration) == meter_length` per voice, with exact Fraction values and explicit rests; verify sounding lengths do not exceed notated/tied windows.
- At each chord change, inspect every active held note using half-open intervals `[onset, release)`; no false carryover for notes ending exactly at boundary.
- Octave simultaneity: equal onset and articulation group; upper/lower octave pitches differ by 12 semitones and 7 diatonic steps only when an octave is explicitly requested.
- Negative cases: missing NCT neighbor => UNKNOWN, unresolved accented dissonance => REVIEW, 2-bar transposition-only => motif-development failure, independent bass copied from soprano => texture-dependence warning, octave chord split into two sequential notes => attack grouping failure.
