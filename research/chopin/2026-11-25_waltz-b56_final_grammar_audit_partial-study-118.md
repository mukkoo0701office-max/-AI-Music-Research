## Reusable generative specification (original proposal; not verified against this work)
1. **Phrase-level planner**: represent form as directed sections with cadence goal, return relationship, and evidence grade. Each two-bar window in 3/4 has exactly 6 quarter units per monophonic voice; use rational onset/duration and explicit rests. Never enforce a repeated three-note cell. Build phrase contour and harmonic goal before ornament placement.
2. **Independent layers**: (a) harmonic spans as contiguous, nonoverlapping half-open intervals per harmonic layer; (b) bass anchor events, chord changes and functional trajectory; (c) melody/inner-voice attacked events; (d) dependent ornament events. Polyphonic piano writing requires voice IDs independent of staff. Store written spelling separately from sounding pitch and distinguish tied continuation from attack.
3. **Two-bar variation**: compare attacked pitches by chromatic and diatonic interval sequences, rhythm vectors, metric accent, harmonic function and cadence target. Label EXACT_REPEAT, TRANSPOSITION, TONAL_SEQUENCE, RHYTHMIC_VARIATION, INTERVAL_VARIATION, or UNRESOLVED. A contour-only comparison is insufficient; diatonic sequence can alter semitone distances without losing tonal interval identity.
4. **Non-chord-tone audit**: resolve harmony at event onset AND at resolution. Classify chord tone, passing, neighbor, suspension, retardation, appoggiatura, anticipation, escape or UNKNOWN, using approach/resolution intervals, metrical weight, tie/preparation and voice identity. A dissonance with no verified harmonic timeline must remain UNKNOWN. Explicitly distinguish a tied suspension from an accented reattack.
5. **Subdivisions**: sixteenth=1/4 and thirty-second=1/8 quarter units. For any subdivision, conserve parent duration and onset coverage. Ornament density depends on phrase role and harmonic stress, not uniform repeated cells. Rests and ties count toward timing but not melodic attacks.
6. **Contrapuntal audit**: check voice crossing, register collision, unresolved accented dissonance, hidden and parallel perfect intervals as context-dependent warnings; do not mechanically reject all perfect intervals. Inspect inner-voice motion and bass continuity across harmony changes.
