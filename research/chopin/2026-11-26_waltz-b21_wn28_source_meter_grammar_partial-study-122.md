## Evidence-gated composition grammar (ORIGINAL proposal, not original-score transcription)
1. **Meter and event clocks**: for 3/8, a bar contains 3 eighth-note units, or 3/2 quarter-note units. Two bars span 3 quarter-note units, not 6. Use Fraction durations; event onset and offset must be measured in a common unit. Each notated voice fills each bar exactly, counting rests. No forced three quarter-note bass/chord attacks as in 3/4 waltzes. Rhythmic surface can divide eighths into sixteenths (1/2 eighth) and thirty-seconds (1/4 eighth), while structural beat grouping remains witness-specific.
2. **Form before surface**: create an abstract section graph with repeat and return nodes; source-derived labels require verified bar boundaries. Generate structural phrase endpoints and cadence targets first. Repeat may alter harmony, accompaniment, register, density or ornament while retaining structural anchors.
3. **Two-bar motif**: represent attacks as (voice, rational onset, duration, spelled pitch, harmonic role, anchor weight, source witness). Compare exact repetition, transposition, intervallic sequence, rhythmic augmentation/diminution, and decorated return separately. Never call contour-only similarity a proven sequence; require at least two anchors and a harmonic path. Permit UNKNOWN if witness data are absent.
4. **Harmonic/nonharmonic analysis**: intervals of harmonic support are witness-local. For every dissonance, test context across preceding and following attacks: passing/neighbor require melodic approach and exit; suspension requires prior consonance, sustained onset across a harmonic change, then resolution; appoggiatura requires new attack and contextual resolution. Store UNKNOWN rather than force classifications when harmony or voice identity is uncertain. Check vertical sonorities at asynchronous attacks and held-note intersections.
5. **Bass and contrapuntal roles**: assign independent bass/inner/top events and phrase-level function; generate chordal attacks only when they do not erase the 3/8 metric identity. Preserve held tones during upper ornamentation. Flag—not automatically forbid—parallel perfect intervals, crossing and accented collisions for harmonic review.
6. **Source variants**: witness_id must be required for every observed event; a generated score may select one coherent witness, or deliberately synthesize variants but must label the latter EDITORIAL_COMPOSITE. Never silently merge distinct autograph/print readings.

## Acceptance tests (SPECIFIED ONLY, NOT EXECUTED)
- 3/8 two-bar synthetic stream totals 3 quarter-note units; a 6-unit stream fails.
- A single eighth subdivided into 2 sixteenths or 4 thirty-seconds preserves rational duration.
- Two voices with simultaneous onsets remain separate in motif comparison.
- Transposition of two-bar anchored events is classified separately from contour-preserving deformation.
- Unprepared tied note cannot be certified as suspension; ambiguous harmony yields UNKNOWN.
- Repeated 3/8 sections with alternate endings preserve independent bar lengths and source witness.
