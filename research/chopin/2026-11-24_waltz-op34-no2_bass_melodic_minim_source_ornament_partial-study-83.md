## Distinct transferable composition principles (PROPOSED, not original Chopin notes)
1. **Two-speed left hand:** Separate `bass_anchor` (harmonic root or pedal) from `sustained_inner_melody` (a long expressive harmonic note). The LH minim is not automatically an inert chord member: it can be an independent melodic event with its own approach, retention and departure. A generator must support a minim sounding through two beats while other accompaniment events occur in another voice. Do not assume every 3/4 bar is root on beat 1 plus identical chords on beats 2/3.
2. **Versioned ornament object:** `{witness,bar,anchor_onset,notated_glyph,grace_count,notated_grace_value,execution_policy,editorial_confidence}`. The editorial recommendation to align the first grace note with bass is a *performance policy*, not a license to silently alter notation. A double grace figure can be related in function to a mordent without equating the textual witnesses. Source alternatives remain branches, not a merged Frankenstein score.
3. **Cross-boundary voice continuity:** a tie extends one pitch event; a slur articulates a phrase; reattack of the same pitch creates a new onset. At harmonic boundaries, preserve the true sustained sounding set and audit preparation/resolution. The LH tie variant in bar 23 must be tied to its witness; do not spread it automatically to every analogous passage.
4. **Two-bar phrase grammar:** create a pair with distinct `bass_motion`, `melodic_goal`, `accent_profile`, `inner_voice_sustain`, `ornament_policy`, `cadence_state`. In the second unit preserve at least one identity (contour or rhythm) but change a consequential parameter (harmonic goal, voice exchange, register or cadence). A trivial three-note cell repeated four times with no larger harmonic purpose is flagged.
5. **Non-chord tone audit:** classify `passing`, `neighbor`, `appoggiatura`, `suspension`, `anticipation`, or `unresolved/unknown` using approach/departure, metrical accent, chord-span boundaries and sounding overlap; classifications are contextual candidates, not proof from pitch class. An accented dissonance may be intentional and should be reported, not automatically replaced with a chord tone.
6. **Exact rhythmic ledger:** use rational onsets/durations per voice; 3/4 = six eighths = twelve sixteenths = twenty-four thirty-seconds. Unmeasured grace notes have zero *notated* bar occupancy and a separate performance scheduling policy. Do not equate notated grace-value changes across witnesses with a fixed absolute playback duration.
