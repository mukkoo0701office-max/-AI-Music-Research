### 1. Form and texture as a hierarchical state machine

### 2. Distinct clocks and nonchord-tone audit
Store notes as (voice, start Fraction, end Fraction, spelled pitch, attack, tie linkage, witness). Store harmony spans separately. Partition each sounding note at all harmony boundaries; classify every nonchord span as prepared suspension, passing, neighbor, appoggiatura, anticipation, pedal, or UNKNOWN. A suspension requires consonant preparation *before* the boundary, actual continuity across it, dissonance after it, and a supported stepwise resolution. Never accept “same pitch class” or a tie flag alone as proof. Appoggiaturas may be accented/unprepared, so an unconditional “all dissonance forbidden” check is incorrect. If score source does not support a classification, emit UNKNOWN and retain evidence.
For 3/4, a monophonic voice's notes and rests must fill 3/4 per bar; simultaneous voices each have their own 3/4 clock. The original notation's hand assignment must not be confused with contrapuntal voice identity. For 16ths and 32nds use exact Fraction times, never floating-point onset comparisons.

### 3. Two-bar development instead of cell repetition
Represent A as two bar-level events with (rhythmic accents, contour, harmonic target, bass trajectory, density, cadence). Generate B with at least one identity-preserving invariant (e.g. accented contour or cadence function) AND at least one phrase-level transformation (e.g. rhythmic displacement, register reversal, different bass destination, delayed cadence, density ramp). Literal reprise is allowed only as an explicitly labeled exception. Compare the *whole two-bar trajectory*; a different 3-note chromatic neighbor is not sufficient variation. Musical validity still requires local harmony and voice-leading checks.

### 4. Ornaments and edition awareness
Keep written ornament glyph, auxiliary notes, start timing, harmonic context, witness and editor-supplied performance realization separate. Editorial commentary about anticipated arpeggiation at bars 1–9 is an interpretation constraint, not license to rewrite score onsets as historical facts. Never infer trill beginnings in one bar solely from an analogous bar with changed harmony. Preserve uncertain witness variants instead of averaging them.
