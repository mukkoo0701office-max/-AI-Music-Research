## Original algorithm proposal (NOT assertions about Chopin's specific notes)
- Separate **harmonic skeleton** H(t), **melodic anchors** A(t), **bass targets** B(t), **texture carrier** C(t), and **surface events** E(t). H and B must be planned before dense subdivision. Every generated event stores source status (verified source / inferred / original synthetic).
- Texture carrier is an independent timeline indicating which voice holds the dense stream; hand/voice transfer is not inferred from registral location. Allow handover overlap or punctuating chord only where voice allocations and durations remain legal.
- Construct 2-bar phrase grammar with explicit target cadence and at least one nontrivial development: change in interval contour, harmonic destination, rhythmic placement, density, or register. Reject simple three-note cell looping as a completed phrase.
- Each nonchord tone carries a candidate type and proof fields: prevailing chord with spelling, metric accent, preparation, approach, resolution, and source confidence. A passing tone requires stepwise approach and departure in a consistent direction; a neighbor returns to anchor; a suspension needs a prepared tied pitch across a harmony change and stepwise resolution. If information is missing, ABSTAIN rather than infer from pitch class alone.
- Subdivision uses exact rational times. 16ths and 32nds are generated by partitions of beat spans with total duration preserved. Tuplets require explicit n:m and span, never deduced from printed numeral alone.
- Counterpoint audit distinguishes voice-leading between structural anchors from simultaneous decorative attacks; flag consecutive perfect intervals only after voice identity and metrical alignment are established.
- Require phrase-level variation: compare interval contour signatures of consecutive cells, density curves and harmonic arrivals; do not use diversity score alone to claim musical quality.
