## Core analytical issue
In final 2/2 notation, RH eighth-note triplets (12 events per 2/2 bar) and LH quarter-note triplets (6 events per bar) share the same underlying 1/12-whole-note onset lattice. The LH onset every two RH attacks is **2:1 aligned**, not a continuously shifting 3:2 cross-rhythm. Do not call it genuine phase-shifting polyrhythm. The 2/4 autograph reported with halved values requires a **notation-version-aware normalization**: halve every written duration while preserving relative rhythm, grouping, and total two-beat measure *in the relevant edition*. Do not indiscriminately rescale the modern score.

## Proposed ORIGINAL compositional engine (not transcription)
1. Plan formal sections, cadence destinations, harmonic rhythm, bass anchors and melodic peak trajectory BEFORE figuration.
2. Represent a written event with (edition, voice, onset Fraction, written_duration Fraction, tuplet_ratio, tie_group, pitch, accent, source_status). Normalize to sounding whole-note durations per edition, retain the source notation for audit. For modern 2/2 use 12 rational subdivisions per bar; avoid assuming all attacks occur on this lattice when ornaments or finer subdivisions enter.
3. Independently generate upper stream U (12 onset opportunities/bar) and lower stream L (6 onset opportunities/bar). For each two-bar gesture, vary contour, apex location, density, bass destination and harmonic target; preserve a recognizable rhythm or interval signature. The U/L alignment is an option, not an obligation for original compositions.
4. Assign each note to structural chord tone, contextual passing/neighbor/appoggiatura/suspension/anticipation, or unresolved. Inspect preceding and following notes *in its own voice*, local metric weight, preparation, and actual harmonic boundary; allow ambiguous readings. Long notes are segmented for harmonic audit without adding attacks.
5. Voice leading: independently constrain bass leap, upper melodic apex, and inner voice continuity. Treat dissonance by register and resolution context, not automatic pitch-class rejection. Add expressive shaping at phrase scale; do not infer an accent on every triplet.
6. Tests: per-bar sum and tuplet arithmetic, RH/LH onset correspondence, version scaling round-trip, 2-bar transformation trace, tie/sustain across chord boundary, nonharmonic-tone classifier confusion tests, and formal continuity. Reject the three-note-cell repetition as sufficient evidence of musical development.
