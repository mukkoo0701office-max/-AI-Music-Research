## Composition grammar (ORIGINAL PROPOSAL, not a claim about B.44)
**Hierarchical form**: represent sections as nodes (theme_id, key_region, phrase_length, cadence_goal, reprise_of, variation_vector, evidence). The secondary A-B-A-C-A-B-A proposal becomes a *hypothesis to test*, not a hard-coded template. Every return must preserve at least one recognizable motivic invariant while allowing altered register, harmonic rhythm, voice distribution and cadence.
**Harmonic motion**: schedule contiguous harmonic spans as [start,end) rational beat positions. Plan tonal destination at phrase boundaries, then generate bass trajectories with root, inversion, pedal and approach categories. Never infer a modulation from one chromatic note. Confirm a new region via cadence and harmonic persistence.
**Two-bar transformation**: encode a motive as ordered attacks with (voice, onset, duration, diatonic interval, chromatic interval, metric position, harmonic role, contour). Compare two-bar windows by exact rhythm, diatonic transposition, tonal sequence, augmentation, truncation and cadence mutation. Preserve scale-degree identity separately from MIDI semitone displacement. Reject classification based only on a three-note repeated cell.
**Melody/inner voices**: first generate structural targets (3rd/7th or contextually licensed tension) at phrase stress points, then interpolate passing/neighbor motion and independent inner-voice counterlines. Avoid global prohibition on tonic or fifth: these may be appropriate at cadences.
**NCT labels**: CHORD, PASSING, NEIGHBOR, SUSPENSION, RETARDATION, APPOGGIATURA, ANTICIPATION, ESCAPE, UNKNOWN. Require preceding and following sounding event in the same voice, chord at onset and resolution, tie/attack distinction and accent class. For suspension, require preparation and tie across harmonic boundary, followed by step resolution. When evidence is insufficient, output UNKNOWN rather than invent a function.
**Rhythm**: in 3/4, use rational quarter-beat units; 16th=1/4, 32nd=1/8. Every voice's event intervals (including rests and ties) must cover exactly three quarter units per full bar, without unintended overlap. Distinguish attacked-note density from sounding-note density; ornament figures may cross harmonic boundaries and need independent audit.
**Contrapuntal checks**: detect overlapping voice ownership, crossing, repeated register collision, unprepared accented dissonance, parallels/hidden perfect intervals as *review flags*; these are not universal automatic bans.
