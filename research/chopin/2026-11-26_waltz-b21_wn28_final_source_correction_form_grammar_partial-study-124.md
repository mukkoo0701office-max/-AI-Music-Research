Do not learn sectional lengths from the E playback sequence as if unquestionably authentic.

## Original compositional grammar (not a transcription)
1. Source layer: every observed event carries witness_id, edition_layer, bar_id, written_meter, sounding_pitch, spelled_pitch, rational onset/duration, voice_id, chord_group_id, and evidence grade. An editorial expansion is a graph operation, never an unlabelled extra set of source measures.
2. Form layer: phrase nodes have spans, thematic anchors, cadential goals, and repeat edges; the same theme may be realized with different phrase-length articulation and accompaniment, while alternative endings remain separate edges.
3. Harmonic layer: define structural bass and harmonic goals before surface decoration. For substitution, require stable endpoint function and inspect voice-leading distances; alternatives from different witnesses cannot be silently mixed.
4. Motif layer: compare two-bar windows **within a voice**, using interval vector, onset/duration vector, spelling, and anchor map. EXACT requires identity; TRANSPOSED requires constant interval shift (with spelling check); SEQUENCE requires interval/rhythm relation plus a planned harmonic displacement; ORNAMENTED_RETURN preserves anchor order while subordinate attacks change. Similar contour alone => UNCLASSIFIED. Three-note cells alone are insufficient evidence.
5. NCT layer: compute sounding harmonic support at each attack AND each harmony-change boundary under a held note. Passing and neighbor require directional step contexts; suspension requires preparation, carry across harmonic change, and resolution; appoggiatura requires a new accented attack and contextual resolution. Unknown harmony or voice continuity => UNKNOWN, not a forced label.
6. Rhythm layer: 3/8 bar = 3/2 quarter-note units. 16th = 1/4, 32nd = 1/8. Use exact fractions and voice-specific interval coverage; chord notes at identical onset share a chord group. Do not force the 3/4 waltz's three-quarter bass/chord pattern onto this piece.
7. Counterpoint layer: preserve separate inner/bass lines; evaluate vertical intervals at all attack and release boundaries; parallel perfects, accented clashes, and crossings are review flags rather than universal prohibitions.
