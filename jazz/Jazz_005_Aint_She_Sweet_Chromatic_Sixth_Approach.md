### New technique: chromatic upper approach to ii with 6th sonorities
C6 {C E G A} -> C#6 {C# E# G# A#} -> Dm7 {D F A C} -> G7 {G B D F} is an arrangement-specific chromatic approach. The bass can climb C–C#–D, and the upper voices can be revoiced to avoid block parallelism: C6 E4/A4, C#6 E#4/A#4, Dm7 F4/A4 (note E# and F enharmonic) as an **original pedagogical realization**, not a transcription. C#6 is not a conventional leading-tone diminished chord; do not mislabel it as such. A7+5 can introduce F natural (enharmonic #5) before resolving into A7 or D7; note that voicing and resolution depend on context.
